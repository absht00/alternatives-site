2. Sejda PDF (Best Free Web & Light Desktop Tool)

Free / Freemium

Sejda is a fan favorite for anyone who needs to edit text, fill forms, merge multiple PDFs, or compress large files directly in the browser or via their lightweight desktop app. The free tier is generous (up to 200 pages or 50MB and 3 tasks per day).

Pros
  • No registration required for basic web tasks
  • Files are automatically deleted from servers after 2 hours
  • Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, and mobile browsers
Cons
  • Task and page limits on the free tier
  • Not ideal for heavy confidential corporate documents processed offline

3. Apple Preview & LibreOffice Draw (100% Free & Offline)

100% Free Open Source

If you are on a Mac, Apple Preview is already installed and allows you to sign contracts, rearrange pages, combine PDFs, and add annotations instantly for free. For Windows and Linux users, LibreOffice Draw opens PDFs as vector graphics allowing deep text editing without internet access.

Pros
  • 100% free with zero ads, subscriptions, or limits
  • Total privacy: files never leave your local computer
Cons
  • LibreOffice Draw can alter complex formatting on multi-column PDFs
  • Preview is exclusive to macOS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to upload PDFs to free online web converters?

Reputable tools like Sejda automatically delete uploaded documents from their cloud servers within 2 hours. However, for highly sensitive financial statements or legal contracts, we recommend offline tools like Apple Preview, LibreOffice Draw, or licensed desktop apps.

How do I cancel Adobe Acrobat Pro without paying penalty fees?

Adobe typically charges 50% of the remaining contract balance if you cancel an annual subscription mid-term. Pro-tip reported by users: you can switch your subscription to a cheaper monthly plan first (or contact support claiming financial hardship), then cancel immediately after.
